About Adidas

Founded in Germany in 1949 by Adolf Dassler, Adidas has grown into one of the most iconic sportswear brands in the world. Known for performance and style, Adidas is recognized for its legendary three stripes.

Adidas Shoes

Adidas footwear blends cutting-edge technology with sleek design. From the Ultraboost for runners to the timeless Superstar lifestyle sneakers, Adidas delivers comfort, durability, and style worldwide.

Adidas Innovation

Adidas has redefined sportswear with its focus on sustainability and performance. From Boost cushioning technology for energy return to the use of Primegreen recycled materials, Adidas continues to balance innovation with responsibility. Their dedication to athletes and the planet sets them apart in the global sportswear industry.
