About Nike

Founded in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports, Nike has become a global leader in athletic wear. Known for its bold innovation and the iconic Swoosh, Nike inspires athletes and trendsetters around the world.

Nike Shoes

Nike shoes combine performance and style. From Air Max for everyday comfort to Jordan for basketball culture, Nike continues to lead with designs that deliver both function and fashion.

Nike’s Improvements

Nike continues to push boundaries through innovation, sustainability, and athlete-driven design. Technologies like Flyknit reduce waste while providing lightweight performance, and Air Zoom cushioning enhances responsiveness. With eco-friendly manufacturing and bold new styles, Nike remains a pioneer in the sportswear industry.
