About New Balance

Founded in 1906, New Balance has built a legacy of comfort, craftsmanship, and performance. Known for its focus on fit and quality, New Balance has earned a reputation as the choice for athletes and casual wearers alike.

New Balance Shoes

From the timeless 574 to the performance-driven Fresh Foam series, New Balance shoes balance innovation with tradition, offering unparalleled comfort and style.

New Balance’s Improvements

New Balance continues to focus on sustainability, innovation, and community impact. Technologies like FuelCell cushioning boost performance, while eco-friendly initiatives in production showcase their commitment to a better future. With timeless silhouettes and modern engineering, New Balance remains a trusted global brand.
